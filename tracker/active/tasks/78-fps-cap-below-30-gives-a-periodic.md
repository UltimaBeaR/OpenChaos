# 78 — FPS cap below 30 gives a periodic stutter (cap clamped to 30)

- Status: 🔵 open
- Area: platform
- Tags: analysis, bug
- Created: 2026-10-27
- Related: migrated from the old list `archive/known_issues_and_bugs/known_issues_and_bugs.md`

## Description
**Symptom:** at `fps_cap` below ~30 (tested at 10/15/22/25/29) — **a sharp, clearly noticeable stutter about once per ~second**. At 30 and above (30/31/32/40/60/…/unlimited) — perfectly smooth; the boundary is **exactly at 30 fps**. **Current solution (1.0):** when reading `video.fps_cap`, positive values <30 are raised to 30 (the `RENDER_FPS_MIN_CAP` constant in [`game_globals.h`](../../../new_game/src/game/game_globals.h), the clamp in [`host.cpp`](../../../new_game/src/engine/platform/host.cpp); `<=0` = unlimited). We'd like to allow caps below 30 in the future (e.g. 24/25 for a "cinematic" fps or to save power on weak hardware) — but first we need to understand and remove the stutter. **What's been figured out (full profiling by frame phases, with a temporary log):** the stutter is **~60ms during which our thread simply isn't executed** at the frame boundary; ALL our phases on the spike frame are **tiny** (physics ~0.1ms, scene drawing ~2ms, HUD ~0.01ms, present ~0.35ms, glFinish ~4ms, `total`~7ms while `dt`=108ms). So it's **not our CPU code, not GPU load, not the limiter sleep** (busy-wait didn't help), **not sound** (disabling `handle_sfx` didn't help). **Excluded:** DWM composition of windowed mode (it stutters in fullscreen too), monitor frequency (180 Hz — the 30 boundary doesn't depend on it), G-SYNC/VRR (disabling didn't help), NVIDIA "Threaded Optimization" (off didn't help), GPU power-state / "max performance" (didn't help), Windows power-throttling/EcoQoS — tried `SetProcessInformation(ProcessPowerThrottling)` from code (didn't help, code removed). **Key clue:** the problem is **only in our OpenGL build** — for **PieroZ (D3D6) at the same ~22 fps there's NO stutter** (same PC/GPU). So it's about the **frame-handoff path OpenGL → Windows compositor** (the asynchronous work of the driver/scheduler at low fps) which D3D6 doesn't have. **Driver settings that DID affect it:** the **"OpenGL GDI compatibility"** parameter in the NVIDIA panel definitely affects it — at "Auto" the stutter is present; one direct value ("performance priority" or "compatibility") in combination **with some other** settings **removed** the problem **once**. **BUT:** which exact settings and in what combination are needed — **couldn't be established**: **we tried every possible value**, after a reset to default the problem returns at **any** "GDI compatibility" value, a reproducible curing combination was **never found**. **Rejected fix directions (by user decision):** NvAPI (forcing a driver profile from the game) — no, vendor lock-in, cross-platform is needed; reworking present to a flip-model swapchain (D3D/DXGI interop) — no, too much risk/work in the renderer. **Next:** understand the GL→present path itself at low fps (possibly the swap interval / the way of `SwapBuffers` / interop with DWM affects it), find a **cross-vendor** way to remove the stutter — and then lift/lower the 30 clamp.

Old-list status: *Investigate*.
